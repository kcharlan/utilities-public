Method. Inflation compounds at the user's annual rate, converted to an effective periodic rate (APY). · Tax. The Income effective rate applies to recurring investment income and external income. The Asset sale effective rate applies to gross asset-sale proceeds. When sufficient assets exist, the calculator grosses up a cash-buffer shortfall so net sale proceeds fill it: gross sale = shortfall ÷ (1 − rate) for rates below 100%. · Decay. Gross liquidation—not net proceeds—drives principal reduction and sell-off decay. Each principal change adjusts the running investment-income ledger by modifier × income yield × principal change. The modifier in force at that event is locked in; a later income edit resets the yield and running income. · Adjustments. Click + to edit beginning assumptions or ending balances. Closing principal is a valuation, and closing cash is a separate adjustment; neither is a sale or recurring income. · Export. CSV tax_paid is combined tax; income_tax_paid, sale_tax_paid, and net_sale_proceeds are appended. · Termination. The projection stops when monthly expenses or the protected reserve cannot be funded, or at the period cap. · Scope. This planning simplification does not model cost basis, lot selection, account type, capital-gains character, deductions, or jurisdiction-specific rules. It is not tax or investment advice.
