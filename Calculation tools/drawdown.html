Method & scope

Method
Inflation compounds monthly from the annual effective rate. Expenses are funded only from cash above the protected floor; sales seek enough net proceeds to fund expenses and restore it. Required and funded expense, unmet expense, and unmet reserve are reported separately.
Tax
The Income effective rate applies to recurring investment income and external income. The Asset sale effective rate applies to gross asset-sale proceeds. When sufficient assets exist, the calculator grosses up a cash-buffer shortfall so net sale proceeds fill it: gross sale = shortfall ÷ (1 − rate) for rates below 100%.
Income ledger
Gross sales reduce principal. Sales and closing-principal valuations adjust raw investment income by modifier × income yield × principal change, using the modifier in force at each event. Later principal changes can raise or lower that ledger. At zero principal, payable investment income is zero even if raw income remains; an explicit income edit resets raw income and yield.
Assumptions
Only expenses inflate; investment income and external income stay at their nominal amounts. Principal changes only through sales and closing valuations, and cash earns nothing.
Waterline
Each point reruns this scenario from that starting principal and monthly investment income, with every other input and adjustment as set, until the reserve fails or the 1,200-month cap. A point lasts the months its expenses and reserve were fully funded; reaching the horizon exactly counts as lasting it. Adjustments that set principal or investment income turn the map off.
Pins
Beginning assumptions affect the selected month; ending cash and principal are exact closing balances after its expenses and sales, and ending income becomes the next month's baseline. Closing principal is a valuation, and closing cash is a separate adjustment; neither is a sale or recurring income. Move a pin to an unoccupied month or use the Adjustments panel to recover an unreachable pin. Yearly flow edits solve an exact target for the planned group when saved; the saved target is provenance, not a live constraint.
Export
The table rounds money to whole dollars with cents and exact values in cell detail. CSV provides readable cents and exact *_raw Number columns, date/span and start/end application provenance. Readable tax_paid reconciles with its two tax parts; gross sold reconciles with sale tax and net proceeds. Invalid edits keep the last valid view and disable export until corrected.
Termination
The projection stops with reserve failure when an expense or the protected reserve cannot be funded; zero principal or touching the floor alone is not failure. Otherwise it ends at the requested horizon or the 1,200-month cap. A yearly row may cover fewer than 12 actual months if failure occurs or the requested horizon is partial.
Scope
This planning simplification does not model cost basis, lot selection, account type, capital-gains character, deductions, or jurisdiction-specific rules. It is not tax or investment advice.
