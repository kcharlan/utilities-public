Method. Inflation compounds at the user's annual rate, converted to an effective periodic rate (APY). · Tax. The effective rate is applied to the sum of investment and external income before expenses are deducted. · Decay. Investment income reduction is permanent and cumulative; each sale subtracts (modifier × percent of original principal sold) from a running coefficient. The modifier in force at the time of sale is locked in — later modifier changes never re-evaluate past sales. · Pins. Click the + in the left gutter of any row to attach an override. Pins apply forward from the pinned period; past rows are immutable. · Termination. The simulation ends when investments reach zero and the buffer is at or below its floor (or at the period cap, whichever comes first).
