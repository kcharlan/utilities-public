What the data says: release-timing patterns

Descriptive analysis of all 531 dated release events through October 29, 2026. “Major” means flagship + mid tiers. Grouped family releases count as one event; previews, hosted snapshots, and general releases can be separate events.

1. Does Moonshot follow Anthropic by 6–8 weeks?

No consistent 6–8 week pattern. For Moonshot's 11 major releases with a prior Anthropic major, the lags are 14, 90, 50, 31, 38, 64, 4, 16, 10, 25, 25 days. Only 1 of 11 falls in the 42–56 day window. The median is 25 days. These dates alone do not establish whether either lab responds to the other.

2. Google's timing relative to OpenAI

Across 16 Google major releases with a prior OpenAI major, the median lag is 6.5 days (mean 22.4). Gemini 1.5 Pro and Sora were announced on the same day, February 15, 2024; Gemini 2.5 Pro and GPT-4o image generation were also same-day events, March 25, 2025. Astra arrived after Gemini 3.8 Flash, so it does not change that earlier release's follower lag.

3. Cross-lab release clusters

There are 475 same-day cross-lab release pairs and 1998 pairs within 2 days (including same-day pairs). Multiple models on one date contribute multiple pairs, so these are event-pair counts, not distinct days.

Recent releases include Granite Switch 4.1.1 (October 29), Mistral Large 4 (open weights) (October 29), Gemini 4 Carbon (limited preview) (October 28), Nemotron 3.5 Transcribe (October 28), Prism (October 28), Genie 4 (research preview) (October 27), Giotto Alto / Solo (October 27), LFM3 family (October 27), MAI Image 2.7 (preview) (October 27).

7. Interpretation and limits

Release timing alone does not demonstrate competitive response or causation. These descriptive counts do not test statistical significance. Shared launch calendars, selection of notable releases, and multiple related models announced together can all produce apparent timing patterns.

Dates generally represent public announcements; explicitly noted hosted availability dates are used for some API models. Approximate dates are flagged in tooltips. A follower lag uses the most recent same-day or earlier major release; events with no prior leader release are excluded. This is a curated timeline, not an exhaustive model registry. Access can be staged or restricted even after announcement. Verification checked October 30, 2026.
