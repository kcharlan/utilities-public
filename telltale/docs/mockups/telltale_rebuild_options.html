Rebuilding history, one provider or all

For discussion, 2026-10-28. Synthetic values. All three options share one mechanism: each provider keeps its own evaluation progress, so rebuilding Claude Code, or adding it for the first time, re-reads only Claude Code's logs and replays them without notifying, while Codex keeps watching live. The options differ in where the control lives and how much it tells you.

A. One button, the scope is chosen in the confirmation

The Data section keeps one button. Its confirmation asks which provider to rebuild and says what each choice removes. Smallest change; nothing new to learn. Per-provider state (not set up, format problem, history read) stays where it is today: the sidebar and the Collector view.

B. A Providers section, one row each, with its own Rebuild (recommended)

Settings gains a Providers section: one row per provider with its state, its data folder, the client version last seen, how much history Telltale holds, and its own Rebuild. The Data section keeps Rebuild all providers…. A rebuild in progress shows in its row. The row also gives a future provider such as Cowork or ChatGPT Work a place to be switched on, without designing that now.

Rebuild Codex history?

Telltale will re-read the last 30 days of Codex logs. Codex speed history before Jan 01, 2000 is removed. Claude Code isn't touched and keeps watching. Accepted baselines are kept, and no notifications are sent for the history it reads.

For
  • Rebuild sits next to the facts that make you want one: a format problem, a version change, missing history.
  • One home for each provider's state, instead of spreading it over the sidebar, Collector view and Settings.
  • Room for a later "watch this provider" switch (Cowork, ChatGPT Work) without a redesign.
Against
  • A new Settings section to build and test, and snapshots to add.
  • Two rebuild buttons in Settings, so each must say exactly what it touches.

C. Rebuild from the provider's own place in the sidebar

Selecting a provider's name in the sidebar opens a provider page (today the names are headings only): its state, history, models at a glance and Rebuild. Settings keeps only Rebuild all providers. The most direct, but it adds a page type and a navigation level for something done rarely.

When a provider shows up for the first time

After the upgrade that adds Claude Code, the first collector run finds its logs and reads 30 days of history without notifying. Three ways to tell you, from quietest to most deliberate:

1. Quiet It appears in the sidebar and the panel; Events records it. Nothing else.

2. Announce once in the panel (recommended) A standing note, like the lasting-shift prompt, until you dismiss it.
